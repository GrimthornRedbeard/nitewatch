Is this thing working?

    
Fair question. Security software that is doing its job is silent,
      and silent looks exactly like broken.

    

      This runs a drill: it makes NiteWatch raise every kind of warning it knows how to
      raise, all at once, so you can see what each one looks like and read what it means
      before you ever meet one for real. Below is what each will say, and what would actually
      be happening on your computer for it to appear.
    

    

      Nothing bad happens. No file is touched, no program is started, nothing connects
      anywhere. The warnings are marked as a test and you can clear them with one button.
      
What this proves: that the warnings, the advice and the buttons all work.
      What it does not prove: that the watching works — the banner at the top of the
      page is what tells you that.
